Privacy by design

🔒

No account, ever

No login, no profile, no cloud sync.

📵

No internet required

All models run on-device. The only optional network call is checking for a newer version.

Audio stays local

The world,

C Around is an offline accessibility companion that describes what is in front of you, names the people you know, reads signs aloud, monitors sounds, and keeps your emergency information one tap away — without sending a single byte to the internet.

Latest release:

Android 8.0+, arm64. Windows & Linux also available below. Links always point to the newest release.

Download

Android

Recommended

The full C Around experience. Camera awareness, face recognition, sound monitoring, live captions, AAC talk screen, emergency card and navigation — all running on-device with no internet.

  • Camera hazard detection (YOLO)
  • Sound classification (YAMNet)
  • Face recognition & naming
  • Offline speech-to-text captions
  • Emergency medical card
  • AAC phrase bank

Download APK (arm64)

Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer · arm64-v8a · Allow “Install from unknown sources” in your phone’s settings first.

How to sideload the APK
  1. Download the APK to your Android device.
  2. Open Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps.
  3. Allow your browser or file manager to install APKs.
  4. Tap the downloaded file and follow the prompts.

Windows

x64

Includes all features except face recognition and YAMNet sound classification (camera-based features work on desktop webcam). The optional Moondream vision model runs locally via a bundled llama-server.

Everything works offline

No account. No subscription. No upload. All inference runs on your device using bundled ONNX models.

👁️

Camera Awareness

Continuous YOLO object detection speaks hazards aloud — cars, stairs, people, bikes — with direction and confidence.

🔊

Sound Monitor

YAMNet classifies 521 audio classes and alerts you to sirens, alarms, knocking, breaking glass and more — even in silent mode.

👤

Face Recognition

Enrol the people you know. C Around names them when they appear in frame, with direction: “Sara is on your left.”

📝

Live Captions

Offline Whisper speech-to-text transcribes speech in real time, with full history stored locally. Nothing is sent anywhere.

💬

Talk & AAC

A phrase bank of quick-tap cards for non-verbal communication. Tap a card to speak it, or type anything and send it to the voice.

🚨

Emergency Card

Your medical information and emergency contact shown full-screen with one tap — no unlock required in an emergency.

🗺️

Navigate

Save locations and items. Find where you left your keys, or get spoken walking directions to a saved place.

🌐

Four Languages

Full interface in English, Arabic (RTL), French and German. Change language in Settings — no restart needed.
